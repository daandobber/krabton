Waves: wavetable data for Krabton's Waves instrument.

waves_data.bin holds 128 single-cycle waves of 600 samples each as
little-endian signed 16-bit integers, 16 banks of 8 waves in the order
below. Each wave has its DC offset removed and is normalized to peak;
some are rotated (circularly shifted) or inverted, which does not
change their sound, so that neighbouring waves morph smoothly.

Credit: the waveforms come from the Adventure Kid Waveforms (AKWF) by
Kristoffer Ekstrand: https://github.com/KristofferKarlAxelEkstrand/AKWF-FREE
